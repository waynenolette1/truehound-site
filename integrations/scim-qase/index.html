Run a free scanSee the live demo

Read and fixed through the app's SCIM 2.0 API, with a SCIM token. Truehound removes access through the app's API after your approval, then reads the app again to prove it is gone.

What Truehound reads

Users, their status and roles, through the app's SCIM 2.0 API.

Not provided by Qase

  • Last sign-in: not provided by SCIM.
  • Second factor: not provided by SCIM.

What it can fix

Remove a person from Qase through its SCIM API after your approval, then read Qase again to prove they are gone.

Set up

  1. 1In Qase open the Workspace area, go to the SCIM page and create a new SCIM API Token, sent as a Bearer token.
  2. 2Paste the SCIM token here. It stays in this browser and is sent only to the app, through Truehound's relay.
Qase's own guide

Plans needed

  • Reading: Enterprise subscription
  • Removing: Enterprise subscription
  • Last sign-in: not provided

Every connection

  • Read-only until you approve a change
  • Every change sealed in the Proof log
  • Apps not checked are listed as not checked
