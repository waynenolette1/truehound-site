Security and trust

Built to be trusted with the keys.

A tool that can see who has access has to be careful with it. Here is exactly what Truehound keeps, where, and for how long. The trust center is open to anyone, with no form to fill in.

Open the trust centerSecurityPrivacyAI disclosure

  • Credentials only if you choose

    A credential is saved only when an admin chooses to, encrypted with a key derived for your workspace, and only its last four characters are ever shown again. The free scan never stores a token.

  • Read-only by default

    Every app connects with read access first. Write access is asked for only when you turn on fixes for that app.

  • Nothing changes without you

    Every removal waits for a person to confirm it. Truehound never decides someone left: your HR system, identity provider or your own list does.

  • Proof you can check

    Every scan and every change is sealed in a SHA-256 hash chain. Verify it yourself on the Proof page, and export it with the report.

  • Clear data retention

    Scan history and the alert log are kept 90 days, sign-in links 24 hours. The proof chain is kept for the life of your workspace, because it is the proof.

  • Secrets stay secret

    A secret found in code is shown as its file, line and a masked preview. The value is never stored or sent anywhere.

When access outlives the job

Real cases, from federal prosecutors and the companies involved. Each link goes to the source. None of them is a Truehound customer.

  • March 2020

    Fired on a Wednesday. His bank's code repositories were deleted that night.

    How the access survived: His account was not disabled when he was fired. It stayed live until the next day.

    At least 220,621 dollars in damage; 24 months in prison.

    Source: DOJ, N.D. Cal.

  • May 2021

    "They didn't revoke my access so I deleted." 21 GB, gone in about 40 minutes.

    How the access survived: Someone asked the outside IT firm to disable her access. It was never done.

    20,433 files and 3,478 folders deleted, including mortgage applications.

    Source: DOJ, E.D.N.Y. complaint

  • August 2018

    Taken off the project. Months later he deleted 1,200 of 1,500 accounts.

    How the access survived: A contractor who had been removed could still reach the company's server.

    The company shut down for two days; 567,084 dollars in restitution.

    Source: DOJ, S.D. Cal.

    One access key sat in a public GitHub repo for almost five years.

FAQ

Before you paste a token.

Something else? A person reads every email to truehoundhq@truehound.io.

Where do my app credentials go?
Nowhere unless an admin chooses to save one. A saved credential is encrypted with a key derived for your workspace, only its last four characters are ever shown again, and you can remove it on Setup at any time. The free scan never stores a token.
How do you know who left?
Your HR system or identity provider says so: a leaving date in BambooHR, Rippling or Deel, or an account suspended in Google Workspace, Okta or Entra. With neither connected, you add the list yourself. Truehound never decides on its own that someone left.
What can it change?
Only what you approve. Where an app's API allows it, Truehound removes the account or its keys; where it does not, you get the link and the steps, and the next read checks the account is gone. Every removal is recorded in a tamper-evident log.
What does it cost?
Quarter Pack $399 once for one access review. Team $299 a month or $2,990 a year, four reviews a year. Starter $149 a month. Flat, never per seat. The live demo is free. Pricing
What does it do when I am not looking?
If you choose to store a read-only GitHub token, it is kept encrypted on our server and your organization or account is re-scanned every 6 hours. The results update in the app. It does not send email or Slack alerts yet, and it never removes anyone without your approval.
Can I use it for a SOC 2 access review?
Yes, as evidence. A review lists every account in every connected app with a decision, a reason, the reviewer and the date, and the evidence pack adds the population, the revokes and how each one was verified, with a hash of every file. Your auditor decides what it covers.
Is this a full security program?
No. Truehound checks the access your connected apps show it. It is not a substitute for a full security program and it does not prevent every breach. It closes the gap that the cases above came through: access nobody took back.
