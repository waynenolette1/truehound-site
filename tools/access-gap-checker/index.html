Free tool

How exposed are you to people who left?

25 checks across seven dimensions, about five minutes, no signup. You get a score for each dimension, the gaps that matter and the fix for each, and you can save the result as a PDF or a link. A self-check, not an audit, and it uses only your answers: no comparison with other companies. Your answers stay in your browser.
