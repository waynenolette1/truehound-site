How to remove a former employee from Microsoft 365 and Entra ID

Sources checked 22 October 2026. 6 minute read.

The short answer

Reset the person's password and sign them out of all sessions first, because blocking sign-in alone can take up to 24 hours to take effect. Then block sign-in, wipe or block their devices, convert the mailbox to a shared mailbox or forward it, and give their manager access to their OneDrive. Remove licenses once the data is safe. Deleting the user keeps them restorable for 30 days, after which their OneDrive and mail are gone.

Cut access first

  1. Step 1. Reset the password and sign out of all sessions[2]

    In the Microsoft 365 admin center, go to Users, then Active users, select the person and choose Reset password. Then, on the Account tab, choose Sign out of all sessions.

    Microsoft says the user is asked to sign in again within an hour, or when they leave the page they are on, because an access token lasts an hour. Outlook on the web may not sign them out until they switch apps or refresh.

  2. Step 2. Block sign-in[2],[7]

    In Active users, select the person, choose Block sign-in, tick Block this user from signing in and save. In the Microsoft Entra admin center the same switch is Account enabled, under Entra ID, then Users, then the user's Account status.

    Microsoft warns that blocking can take up to 24 hours to take effect, which is why the password reset comes first.

  3. Step 3. Revoke refresh tokens with PowerShell[7],[8]

    For a scripted or bulk offboarding, use Microsoft Graph PowerShell. Update-MgUser -UserId ID -AccountEnabled:$false disables the account, and Revoke-MgUserSignInSession -UserId ID invalidates every refresh token issued to apps for that user, and their browser session cookies, by resetting signInSessionsValidFromDateTime to now.

    A user principal name such as dana@example.com works as the user ID.

  4. Step 4. Block mail apps in Exchange Online[2]

    In the Exchange admin center, open Recipients, then Mailboxes, select the mailbox, choose Manage email apps settings and turn off every protocol: Outlook desktop, Exchange web services, mobile, IMAP, POP3 and Outlook on the web.

Revoking sessions stops new tokens from Entra ID. Apps that issue their own session token keep it until it expires or the app ends it, so deprovision the person in those apps too.

Wipe and block their devices

  • Phone with company mail: in the Exchange admin center, open the mailbox, choose Manage mobile devices, select the device, choose an option such as Account Only Remote Wipe Device, then Block access.
  • Devices managed in Intune: in the Intune admin center, go to Devices, then All devices, open the device and choose Remove data, then Wipe. This resets it to factory settings and cannot be undone. On unmanaged devices you can wipe only the company data in managed apps.
  • Microsoft notes that an Intune wipe needs the device to be connected to the internet. An offline device still has its local data.
  • Disable the person's registered devices in Entra ID too, with Get-MgUserRegisteredDevice and Update-MgDevice -AccountEnabled:$false.[3],[10],[7]

Keep their mail and OneDrive

  1. Step 1. Convert the mailbox to a shared mailbox, or forward it[4]

    A shared mailbox keeps all existing mail and calendar items for several people to read, and needs no license while it stays under 50 GB. Forwarding only sends new mail on.

    Do not delete the account if you forward mail or keep a shared mailbox. The account anchors both.

  2. Step 2. Give the manager their OneDrive[5]

    In Active users, open the person, choose OneDrive, then Create link to files under Get access to files to copy or move what you need. To hand the whole OneDrive to someone else, a SharePoint admin adds them as a site collection administrator of that OneDrive under User profiles.

    After you delete the account, you have 30 days by default to reach their OneDrive. You can change that with the OneDrive retention setting for deleted users.

Remove licenses and roles

In Active users, open the person, go to Licenses and apps, clear the licenses and save. Remove any holds first. Then lower the license count under Billing, Your products, if you will not reassign the seat.

When a license is removed, the person's mail, contacts and calendar are kept for 30 days and then permanently deleted. If you need them for legal reasons, put a hold on the mailbox before deleting the user.

If the person held admin roles, take them off too, and check apps and groups they owned before you delete the account.[6]

Delete the user, and the 30 day window

In the Microsoft Entra admin center, go to Entra ID, then Users, select the person and choose Delete user. They move to Deleted users, where you can restore them for 30 days. Their licenses become available straight away.

If your accounts sync from on-premises Active Directory, delete the user there instead; Microsoft 365 cannot delete or restore synced accounts. In a hybrid setup, Microsoft recommends disabling the account in Active Directory and resetting its password twice.[9],[1],[7]

Questions

Does blocking sign-in end their current sessions?
Not right away. Blocking can take up to 24 hours. Reset the password and choose Sign out of all sessions, or run Revoke-MgUserSignInSession, to force a new sign-in.
Do I need to keep paying for a license to keep their mail?
Not if you convert the mailbox to a shared mailbox under 50 GB. Larger shared mailboxes need a license.
How long can I restore a deleted user?
30 days from the Deleted users page. After that their OneDrive and mail content is gone unless you changed retention or placed a hold.
Does removing them in Entra ID remove them from GitHub or Slack?
Only for apps provisioned from Entra ID. Apps with their own logins or their own tokens need their own offboarding; see /guides/remove-user-from-github-organization.
What about apps that use their own session?
Entra ID cannot revoke a session token another app issued. Deprovision the user in that app, or use automatic provisioning so it happens for you.
