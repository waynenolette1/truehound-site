Access reviews and audit evidence

Finish the access review, with the evidence sealed.

Start a campaign, let reviewers keep or revoke each line, and finish with a pack your auditor can check for themselves.

What you get

  1. 01

    Review campaigns

    Choose who reviews what and by when. Run it once or on a schedule, in full or only what changed. Every campaign shows its progress, and accepted risks sit in a register with expiry dates.

    Open it in the live demo
  2. 02

    Low-risk lines kept by your rules

    Auto-certify keeps the low-risk lines inside rules an admin sets, and leaves every flagged line to a person. Each automatic decision names the rule, is sealed in Proof, and can be undone.

    Open it in the live demo
  3. 03

    A sealed evidence pack

    One ZIP laid out the way auditors ask: the population per app, every decision with its reason, every revoke and whether a re-read confirmed it. A manifest lists each file's SHA-256, so the pack can be re-checked.

    Open it in the live demo
  4. 04

    Controls that run alongside

    Separation of duties is checked on review lines and access requests. Evidence expiry and a SIEM stream of the Proof log are set in one place.

    Open it in the live demo
