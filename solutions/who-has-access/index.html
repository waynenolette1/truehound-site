Who has access, and why

Who has access, and why.

Ask about a person or an app and get one answer across everything you have connected, with the reason it was granted when there is one on record.

What you get

  1. 01

    Everyone, across every connected app

    One row per person, with each account matched and the evidence for the match. Switch to accounts, or to one row per app.

    Open it in the live demo
  2. 02

    Every path, with the reason

    Each person's access paths show the request, the matching birthright rule, or the review keep behind them. With none of these, the path says "No recorded reason". Nothing is guessed.

    Open it in the live demo
  3. 03

    Ask in plain language

    Ask who has access, when it changed and why it was granted, with the answer cited. Roles in each app are explained in plain words.

    Open it in the live demo
  4. 04

    A page for each person

    Employees can see their own access and ask for what they need.
