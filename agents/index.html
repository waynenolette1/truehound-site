  1. Agents

The agent library

23 agents. One job each. Every move on the record.

Each agent below is code that runs today, listed from the library itself, never from a slide. Each has an on and off switch, rules you can read, a line it will not cross, and a sealed log of what it did. 11 of them are watchers that stay off until an admin switches them on.

Where they run

  • AutopilotFive of the agents are Autopilot's jobs. Each one is off, prepares changes for a person, or acts alone inside its rules, under 5 rules no setting can loosen and one kill switch.
  • The helpdeskAn employee asks for access in their own words. The helpdesk agent files it as that person and resolves it inside the approval rules, or hands it to the admins with the reason.
  • MCP hooksThe same idea pointed at your own AI agents: a check before each tool call in five AI clients, and a record of every call.

How they hand work on

Each line is an agent's own record, with its receipt. In every Autopilot pass the Triage agent ranks new findings first, then the Leaver agent and the Stale access remover act on them; those lines are linked. The watchers each work on their own.

Autopilot

  • Triage agentGives every new finding a priority (now, today or this week) and the reason, so the queue is in order before anyone opens it.Every 15 minutes, from the server's cron, while Autopilot is on.
  • Stale access removerRemoves dormant accounts, unexpected guests and the agent's detections in the apps you allow, then re-reads the app to prove each one is gone.Every 15 minutes, from the server's cron, while Autopilot is on.
  • Routine request granterGrants requests inside the approval step's rules for a set time, re-reads the app to prove it, and removes the access on its own when the time is up.Every 15 minutes, from the server's cron, while Autopilot is on.
  • Review pre-fill and chase agentPre-fills every open review line with keep, revoke or a person should look, citing the facts, and chases reviewers who have not answered.Every 15 minutes, from the server's cron, while Autopilot is on.
  • Leaver agentRemoves a leaver's accounts, re-reads each app to verify, reclaims the seats with the saving, and files the evidence.Every 15 minutes, from the server's cron, while Autopilot is on.

Requests

  • Helpdesk agentReads an access ticket in the employee's own words (Slack /access or the app), files the request as that person, and resolves it inside the approval step's rules; anything else goes to the admins with the reason.On every ticket.
  • JIT expiry auditorAudits every time-bound grant: one still held after its approved time ran out is removed the way the request removes it, and the app is re-read.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.

Spend

  • License reclaim agentFinds idle seats from each app's own activity, asks each holder whether they still need it, reminds them, and removes the released or unanswered seats under the rule's approval, then re-reads the app and records the saving.Hourly per license rule, from the server's cron.

Reviews

  • Review auto-certify agentKeeps the low-risk lines of an open access review inside rules an admin sets, citing each line's facts, and leaves every flagged or unclear line to a person.When an admin runs it on an open review.
  • Evidence pack builderFiles the evidence pack (population, decisions, removals and a manifest with every file's SHA-256) for each signed access review that has none yet.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.

Discovery

  • OAuth analystGives every third-party app someone granted (Google or Microsoft sign-in grants, apps and bots in connected apps) a verdict of permit, review or revoke, with its reasons and the gaps in what was read.When an admin opens it; observe mode by default.
  • OAuth grant hygieneFinds third-party apps, bots and tokens a connected app reports with broad access, and files their removal where Truehound can run it, or tells the admins.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.
  • New app owner finderFor each app people started signing in to that has no owner in Discovery, asks the person who uses it most to own it, and tells the admins when nobody answers.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.

AI and non-human identities

  • Key and service-account hunterReads AI API keys, service accounts, seats and agents from OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub Copilot, Cursor and the agent builders every day, with each owner and last use, flags those whose owner left or that have no owner, and revokes from the registry with a re-read.Daily, from the server's cron, for each connected provider.

Lifecycle

  • Role mining agentProposes access bundles from the access people hold now, by department and title, names who holds what, and turns a proposal into a birthright template in one click.When an admin opens Workflows, Role mining.
  • Leaver checkerFinds people the identity source or HR marks as gone who still have an active account in another app, and files each removal for approval.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.
  • Contractor and end-date expiryFinds people whose contract or employment end date has passed but who are not marked as left and still hold active accounts, and chases their manager (or the admins) until it is closed.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.

Connectors

  • Connector upkeep agentChecks each approved custom connector against the app itself and its API document every day; on drift it drafts a new version, runs the contract test, and files it for a person to approve.Daily per connector, from the server's cron.

Security

  • MFA gap chaserChases every admin the app itself reports without a second factor until they turn it on, then tells the admins about anyone who has not.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.
  • Dormant admin removerFinds admin accounts nobody has used for the days you set and files the removal of the admin role (or the account) for approval.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.
  • Device posture chaserChases each person whose managed device fails disk encryption, screen lock, malware protection or the firewall, as Jamf Pro, Kandji or Intune report it.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.
  • Vendor breach responderWatches Have I Been Pwned's public breach list for the vendors your people sign in to, tells the admins who uses a breached one, and asks each of those people to change their password there.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.

Governance

  • SoD conflict watcherChecks everyone's access against your separation-of-duties rules (ship code and run production, hire and grant access, book and pay) and tells the admins about every new conflict.Daily from the server's cron once switched on, or now from this page.
