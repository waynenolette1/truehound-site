Spend agent

License reclaim agent

Finds idle seats from each app's own activity, asks each holder whether they still need it, reminds them, and removes the released or unanswered seats under the rule's approval, then re-reads the app and records the saving.

When:
Hourly per license rule, from the server's cron.
Where:
Runs on the team server, page License rules

Rules it follows

  • A seat is idle only past the rule's inactive days, read from the app's latest scan.
  • Removals are filed for approval; hands off only under two admins' standing approval of that exact rule.
  • A saving is recorded only with a known seat price, never a guessed one.

Lines it never crosses

  • Every removal is re-read in the app before it counts.

What needs a person

  • Removals are filed for approval; hands off only under two admins' standing approval of that exact rule.

The proof it leaves

  • Every action is sealed in Proof, the workspace's hash-chained log, as a license campaign event.
  • A change counts only after a fresh read of the app agrees it happened.
  • Switching it on or off is sealed in Proof too, with who did it.
