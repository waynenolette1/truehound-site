Truehound vs AccessOwl

AccessOwl is a strong Slack-first access tool with public pricing. Truehound is flat-priced, starts from who left in your identity provider, and checks every removal again.

The short answer

Choose AccessOwl if you want access requests, approvals and onboarding run inside Slack across hundreds of apps. Choose Truehound if you want a flat price, reviews that schedule themselves, and offboarding that starts when someone is suspended in Google or Entra.

Where AccessOwl is stronger

  • Access requests and approvals, onboarding and provisioning, not only offboarding.
  • A long list of integrations, including ones driven by a service account for apps without an API.
  • Slack-native: approvals, reminders and tasks happen where the team already works.
  • Completed reviews sync to Vanta automatically.
  • More integrations today: AccessOwl lists more than 400. Truehound connects far fewer, and says which.
  • Its trust center lists SOC 2. Truehound holds no SOC 2 report.
  • Public pricing and a self-serve trial.

Where Truehound is different

  • Flat price, never per seat. AccessOwl charges per user with a monthly minimum.
  • Quarterly reviews on a schedule; AccessOwl's docs say every review is started by hand.
  • A Google or Entra suspension starts offboarding; in AccessOwl it marks the user inactive.
  • Removals are checked by reading the app again, so nothing is closed as removed while it still exists.

Questions

Does AccessOwl schedule recurring access reviews?
Its docs say not today: each campaign is started manually, and they suggest setting your own reminder.
Which is cheaper?
It depends on headcount. AccessOwl's access reviews cost $6 a user a month with a $250 minimum, so the bill grows with your team. Truehound is one flat price.
