Comparison

Truehound vs Nudge Security

Nudge Security is excellent at finding the apps nobody told IT about. Truehound is narrower: it works on the apps you connect, and removes access itself.

The short answer

Choose Nudge if your biggest problem is not knowing which SaaS apps people signed up for. Choose Truehound if you know your apps and need leavers removed, with proof, on a monthly price. For apps outside single sign-on, Nudge asks the app's owner to remove the account; Truehound removes it through the app's API where one exists.

Where Nudge Security is stronger

  • Discovery: Nudge finds apps from signup emails, OAuth grants and a browser extension. Truehound does not read email and has no browser extension.
  • A mature offboarding playbook that locks the person out of Google or Microsoft, transfers critical resources and revokes OAuth grants.
  • A trial with no sales call and no payment details, which Truehound does not offer (we offer the free scan and the live demo instead).
  • Flat pricing up to 150 users, with every feature in every tier.

Where Truehound is different

  • It removes access through each app's API where one exists, instead of asking someone else to.
  • It reads the app again after removing, so the proof is the app's own answer.
  • Monthly billing, cancel in the product. Nudge's published plans are billed annually.
  • GitHub depth: deploy keys, outside collaborators, webhooks, public repos and secrets in code.
