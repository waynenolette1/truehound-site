Truehound vs Josys

Josys is a broad identity governance platform with automated reviews and offboarding. Truehound is smaller and flat-priced, and it checks each removal by reading the app again.

The short answer

Choose Josys for a wide governance platform: identity and app discovery, access requests, scheduled access review surveys, leaked credential monitoring and lifecycle policies, quoted by number of identities. Choose Truehound for a self-serve, flat-priced access audit that starts with GitHub and proves each removal.

Where Josys is stronger

  • Identity and app discovery from login data and browser activity, including non-human identities and AI agents.
  • Access reviews as mobile-friendly surveys with a question library, reminders and periodic schedules, ending in revocation.
  • Leaked credential detection matched to your identity inventory.
  • Access requests, a self-service portal and a policy engine for joiners, movers and leavers.
  • A trust page listing SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001. Truehound holds neither.

Where Truehound is different

  • Self-serve: run the free scan in minutes and pay by card when you want names and fixes.
  • A flat price on the website; Josys prices by number of identities after a conversation.
  • After removing, it reads the app again and shows anything still present in red.
