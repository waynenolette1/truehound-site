Is Truehound up?

What we measure, what we do not, and how fast we aim to answer when something is wrong.

Loading the history.

How this is measured: every 15 minutes the connector asks its own database a trivial question and records whether it answered. It does not measure the website, which GitHub Pages serves, your network or any provider we use. A passing check does not mean every feature works, and a day with no squares means no sample was recorded.

Support response targets

First-response targets by email to truehoundhq@truehound.io, in business days (Monday to Friday, US business hours). These are targets, not guarantees, and no credits apply when one is missed.

Support first-response targets by severity
SeverityMeaningTarget
P1, CriticalThe Service is unusable, no workaround1 business day
P2, HighA major function impaired, workaround exists2 business days
P3, MediumA minor function impaired3 business days
P4, LowA question or cosmetic issue5 business days

What we do not promise

We do not publish an uptime percentage as a commitment and we do not pay service credits. The numbers above describe what happened. Read the Service Level Agreement for what we do commit to, and the Trust Center for what we have and do not have.
