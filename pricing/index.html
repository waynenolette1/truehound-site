Pricing FAQ

Questions about the bill.

Can I try it before I pay?
Yes, in the live demo: a made-up company's audit, every screen and every fix (simulated). The live demo is free. Your own audit is paid before it opens: $99 once, or $49 a month to keep watching.
What does the one-time audit cover?
One GitHub org: the scan, the result screen, the one-click fixes and the report, with 30 days from payment to fix things and re-scan. After that your last results stay readable. To scan again, buy another audit or switch to Always watching.
Does my GitHub token leave my browser?
Almost never. The audit runs in your browser and calls GitHub directly. GitHub blocks code and commit search from browsers, so only those requests pass through our relay, which forwards your token to GitHub and never stores or logs it. Any secret we find is shown masked and never stored. Truehound is not a substitute for a full security program.
Can I cancel? What are the terms?
Yes. The one-time audit is paid once and never renews. Always watching renews each month until you cancel; cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you paid for. The Terms of Service cover billing and refunds, and the Privacy Policy says what reaches us.
Where does my data live?
In your browser. The scan runs there against your own GitHub, and your results are kept in this browser's storage, not on our servers. We never store your token. Fixes are made directly on GitHub, only after you confirm each one.
