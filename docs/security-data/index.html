Trust

Security and data handling

Last compared with the product's code 25 October 2026.

Truehound reads people, accounts, roles, status, dates and grants from the apps you connect. It never reads message, document or email bodies. Without an account, what you connect stays in your browser. With a team workspace, the workspace is kept in Cloudflare D1 and every change is sealed in Proof.

What you need, and what is not on yet

This page summarizes the Trust Center, which is the full statement and lists what is in place, what is partly in place and what is not yet. Truehound has no SOC 2 report, does not hold ISO 27001 and has had no independent penetration test yet.

What Truehound reads, and what it never reads

  • From connected apps: people, accounts, roles, status, dates and grants.
  • Never the content of messages, documents or emails. Mailbox discovery is limited to the sender, subject and date.
  • Secret values found in code are masked and never kept.
  • Apps it could not read are listed as not checked. They are never shown as clean.

Where your data lives

Without an account, the token, the audit and the workspace are stored in your browser, not on our servers. The token goes to the vendor, and through our connector only for apps that do not answer browsers or for GitHub code search. The connector relays it per request and does not store or log it.

With a team workspace, the workspace lives in Cloudflare D1. The website is static files on GitHub Pages with no server code. The connector and account backend are a Cloudflare Worker. We run no servers of our own. Every account query is limited to the workspace it belongs to, and automated tests check that a non-member gets not found.

How credentials are protected

  • App credentials an admin chooses to store, including logins for browser playbooks, are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key derived for each workspace from a master secret held only in the connector's secret store, and are bound to their workspace and app. The master key can be rotated. They are never returned to the browser.
  • Login links, sessions, invites, API keys and extension keys are stored only as SHA-256 hashes. An API key is shown once.
  • Your Anthropic key is stored the same way and only its last four characters are ever shown. See AI keys.
  • Truehound has no passwords. You log in by payment, an activation code, an invite link or a passkey, and the session cookie is HttpOnly and Secure.

Control over changes

  • Owner, admin and member roles, with custom roles and per-area read, edit and act permissions, limit who can change what.
  • An approval policy can require one or more approvals, including a second admin, before a fix runs. The person who proposed a fix can never approve it.
  • Every change, approval and consent in a workspace is written to Proof, a SHA-256 hash chain where tampering shows. You can verify it in the app and offline, and stream it to your SIEM.
  • Autopilot's fixed rules and kill switch limit what automation can do. See Autopilot.

How long data is kept

An owner can export everything a workspace holds as one JSON file, and can delete the workspace and everything stored for it in one step. A person can delete their own account. A privacy request is answered within 30 days, and customers are told of a personal data breach within 72 hours.

  • Login links work for 15 minutes and their records are deleted after 24 hours. A session lasts 30 days from your last visit.
  • Scan history is kept 90 days, and the latest successful scan is always kept. Connected-app scans follow the same rule.
  • Review links for reviewers outside Truehound expire 120 days after they are made. Invites expire after 7 days.
  • Browser-extension login sightings are kept 395 days. Demo-form leads are kept 24 months.
  • Restore copies of account data expire within 30 days.

What we do not have yet

  • No SOC 2 report, and no ISO 27001. We do not claim either and will not until an independent auditor issues one.
  • No independent penetration test yet.
  • Backups are automatic, but we have not yet practised a restore. There is no uptime commitment unless an order form gives one.
  • One person, the founder, can reach production.

The Trust Center has the plan for each gap. Security questionnaires are answered directly, usually within 5 business days, at truehoundhq@truehound.io.

Also published

  • Sub-processors, with 30 days' notice before one is added or replaced.
  • The Data Processing Agreement, Master Service Agreement and service levels under Legal.
  • A responsible disclosure policy with safe harbour, acknowledgement within 1 business day, and fix targets of 14 days for critical and 30 days for high issues: security disclosure.
  • The accessibility conformance report and the public status page.
