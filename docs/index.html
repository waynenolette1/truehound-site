How Truehound works.

Truehound finds, removes and proves access for people, service accounts and AI agents. These pages say what each part does today and what has to be set up first. Each one was last compared with the product's code on the date shown.

Looking for the API reference? It is on the Developers page. What shipped, and when, is in the changelog.

Do the work

  • Access reviewsRun a user access review across your connected apps: scope preview, a reason on every line, revoke then verify, sign-off, schedules and the evidence pack.Checked 25 October 2026
  • OffboardingFind everyone who left and still has access, remove them from every app that allows it, check each removal, and keep a receipt with the time it took.Checked 25 October 2026
  • Access requestsSelf-serve access: an employee asks for an app and a role, the owner approves, Truehound grants and checks it, and time-bound access is removed on time.Checked 25 October 2026

AI

  • AutopilotAutopilot works your access queue inside rules you set: triage, removal, routine grants, review pre-fill and leavers. Each change is proven by a re-read.Checked 25 October 2026
  • AI keys (bring your own key)Truehound's AI runs on your own Anthropic key, so the charges go to your account. How to add the key, what is sent, and the page that inventories AI keys people create.Checked 25 October 2026
