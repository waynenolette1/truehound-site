Access reviews

Last compared with the product's code 25 October 2026.

An access review asks the right people to keep or remove each account in each app, records why, removes what they decide to remove, checks that it is gone, and gives your auditor a pack they can verify. Truehound builds the list, suggests a decision with a reason on every line, and refuses to sign off while anything is open.

What you need, and what is not on yet

Reviews, sign-off and the evidence pack work in a workspace and in the live demo. Recurring schedules that launch on the server need a team workspace. Delivery of reviews inside Slack and Microsoft Teams is built but not switched on yet, because the Slack app and the Teams bot are not registered. Reviewers outside Truehound get a link instead.

Start a review

  1. Step 1. Check the scope

    Before launch you see the population: how many accounts across how many apps, how many are flagged, and who the reviewers will be.

    A freshness gate blocks the launch if an app's data is stale, so a review never freezes old data. Apps that could not be read are listed as not checked.

  2. Step 2. Launch

    Launching freezes a snapshot. Later changes in the apps do not move the lines under a reviewer.

Decide every line

  • Each line shows the person, the account, the role, last activity and the source, and the usage band where the app reports one.
  • Each line carries a suggested decision with a one-line reason. Without an Anthropic key the suggestion comes from rules and is labelled as rules. With your own key, Claude can write the reasons, and that is an explicit per-workspace opt-in. See AI keys.
  • A reason is required on every decision, keep or revoke. You can keep many lines at once with one comment.
  • A reviewer cannot decide their own account. Reviewers can delegate a line with a reason, and an admin can reassign lines in bulk.
  • A review can have one to three stages: a manager, an app owner, or named people. Stage two sees stage one's decision and reason.
  • Accept a risk with a reason of 20 or more characters, a named owner and an expiry within a year. When it expires the line reopens by itself.
  • Usage shown beside each line helps you justify a keep or a revoke.

Revoke, then verify

A revoke decision removes the access through the app's API under your approval policy, then Truehound reads the app again. The line counts as done only when the re-read agrees. A cancelled dialog records nothing.

If the re-read still sees the account, or could not run, sign-off stays blocked until you try again, remove it by hand, or accept the risk with a written reason.

A removal done by hand carries a note and up to three screenshots or PDFs and a ticket link. They go into the evidence pack unchanged with their hashes. It stays labelled manual and is not counted as verified until a later read shows the account gone.

For privileged access, Truehound can remove every path to it, whether direct, through a group or through a role, and catch access that is inherited. It previews the effect first.

Sign off

Sign-off is named and carries an attestation. It is refused while any line or revoke is open. Reminders and escalation to the campaign owner are built, but they go out by email, which is not switched on yet.

Schedule reviews

On Review campaigns, a signed-in workspace saves a recurring review on the server: monthly through annual, or every 1 to 365 days, full or delta (only accounts that are new, or whose roles or admin status changed, since the last run).

The server cron launches each schedule on its date with nobody in the app, once per date. It waits, and says why, while another review is open or no app has been read yet. A schedule can end on its due date and skip or revoke undecided lines, with each revoke going through the approval policy. You can also manage schedules as code with Terraform or the API.

The evidence pack

  • A ZIP with a CSV per system, the decisions with reasons, the revocations with their verification state, accepted risks, and escalations.
  • A manifest with SHA-256 hashes of every file, so an auditor can check that nothing changed, and a PDF summary.
  • A read-only auditor link to one pack, valid 1 to 90 days and revocable. Every view, download and check is logged for you.
  • Control mapping to frameworks, and evidence expiry warnings before evidence goes stale.
  • Automatic push of the pack to Vanta or Drata when you sign off, once you connect either on the Controls page.
  • Every launch, decision, delegation, accepted risk and sign-off is sealed in Proof, which you can verify in the app, in the auditor's browser, or offline with the verifier included in the pack.

Truehound does not hold a SOC 2 report or any certification. The pack gives your auditor the evidence of your own review.
