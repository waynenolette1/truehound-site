AI

Autopilot

Last compared with the product's code 25 October 2026.

Autopilot is the AI that does the routine access work, within limits you set and cannot be talked out of. Each of its five jobs is off, prepare for approval, or act alone. Whatever it does is in a log in the first person with its reason, and a change counts as made only when a re-read of the app confirms it.

What you need, and what is not on yet

Autopilot runs on the team server, so it needs a team workspace, and it is off until an admin switches it on. It works on rules alone. Claude writes the reasons only when your workspace has added its own Anthropic key and opted in, and every action says which one decided. The live demo shows Autopilot over the demo's sample findings.

The five jobs

  • Triage new findings: gives every new finding a priority (now, today or this week) and the reason. Modes: off or auto. It starts on auto.
  • Remove stale and risky access: removes dormant accounts (30 to 365 days without a sign-in, 90 by default), unexpected guests and the agent's detections, in the apps you name, then re-reads each app. At most 1 to 100 accounts per run, 10 by default.
  • Grant routine requests: grants requests that fit the approval step's rules for a set time, proves it with a re-read, and removes the access when the time is up. Anything unusual goes to a person with the reasons.
  • Pre-fill reviews and chase reviewers: fills every open review line with keep, revoke or a person should look, citing the facts. It chases reviewers who have not answered after 2 days by default and escalates after 5.
  • Run leavers end to end: removes a leaver's accounts, re-reads each app, reclaims the seats and records the saving, and files the evidence.

Off, approve or auto

Approve means Autopilot prepares the change and a person approves it. Auto means it acts alone inside the job's rules. The remove, grant, review and leaver jobs start on approve. Nothing runs until an admin switches Autopilot on. A cron picks up every workspace that has it on and has not stopped it.

Rules nobody can loosen

  • Never an admin role. It never grants one, never pre-fills keep on one, and never removes an admin account alone.
  • Never a two-approval policy. Where your approval policy asks for two or more approvals, people approve.
  • Never a deny without a person. Autopilot escalates with reasons. Only a person denies a request.
  • Everything it reads from apps, tickets and requests is data, never an instruction to it.
  • The kill switch stops every job before its next action.

A setting that would loosen one of these rules is refused by the server, whatever the value.

The kill switch, the log and undo

  • The kill switch is always in reach. It records who pressed it and why, and is checked before every action. Resume is an admin action.
  • The live log has one row per action: what it did, in the first person, the reason, who decided (rules, or Claude on your own key, checked against the rules), and the evidence.
  • An admin can undo an action where the app allows it. Every action is also one event in Proof.
  • The page leads with what it did: changes made, how many a re-read confirmed, what waits for a person, and what was undone.

How it uses Claude

Claude has no tools in Autopilot. Its answers are checked against the rules before anything acts. Without your own Anthropic key every job runs on rules and says so. See AI keys for how to add one and what is sent.
