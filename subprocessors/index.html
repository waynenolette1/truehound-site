01Current sub-processors

These companies process personal information on Truehound’s behalf. Most are engaged only when a feature is used or switched on; the “When” column says which. Access tokens and secret values are never sent to any of them, except that our connector on Cloudflare relays a token you use for that one request without storing it. Card details go straight from you to Stripe.

02Notice of changes, and your right to object

Before we add or replace a sub-processor that processes customer personal data, we email every customer’s account owners and billing contact at least 30 days before it starts, and post the change here and in the change feed (Atom; any feed reader can follow it).

A customer may object in writing to truehoundhq@truehound.io within that period on reasonable data protection grounds. We will then try to offer a way to use Truehound without that sub-processor; if we cannot, the customer may terminate the affected service and we refund any prepaid fees for the unused period. In an emergency (for example a provider failing), we may replace a sub-processor sooner and will tell customers as soon as we can, with the same right to object.

Each sub-processor is bound by written terms at least as protective as our Data Processing Agreement, and we remain responsible to customers for its work.

03What is not a sub-processor

  • The apps your admins connect (GitHub, Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID, Slack, Okta, your HR, finance, cloud and work tools). Truehound reads them with your own credentials, under your own agreements with those companies.
  • Google Fonts and other font or script networks: our fonts and scripts are served from our own site.
  • Advertising or tracking services: we use none.
