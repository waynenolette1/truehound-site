01Get advance notice

We email subscribers at least 30 days before we add or replace a sub-processor that handles customer personal data. You can object in writing to truehoundhq@truehound.io within that period. In an emergency we may move sooner and will say so. The current providers are on the sub-processors page.

We email a confirmation link first. After that you get a message before a sub-processor is added or replaced, and every message has an unsubscribe link. Nothing else is sent to this address.

Prefer a feed reader? Follow the Atom feed.

02Change log

Sub-processor changes, newest first
DateChange
2026-10-22Register checked against the code: Resend, Microsoft, Finch, Merge and Kombo added, each row says when it is engaged. Resend (account email), Microsoft (Teams review messages) and the HR connection services Finch, Merge and Kombo were in use behind switches but missing from the register, and are added. Every row now names the legal entity, the transfer mechanism and when it is engaged. The register is now also public at /subprocessors.
2026-10-20Register matches the providers the hosted product actually uses. The register now lists exactly the providers hosted Truehound uses: Stripe (billing), Cloudflare (the connector Worker and Workers KV) and GitHub Pages (hosting), plus PostHog and Anthropic only if those features are enabled. Earlier entries described a hosted architecture and placeholder vendors that Truehound does not use, and are withdrawn.
